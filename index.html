How it works

Odds-ratio core. Each team's offense and defense is measured on eFG%, turnover rate, offensive-rebound rate, free-throw rate and FT%, adjusted for opponents and compared with the D1 average on the odds scale (log5 / odds-ratio): odds(expected) = odds(offense) × odds(opp defense) ÷ odds(D1 average), plus a home-court term. Possessions come from an additive tempo model (team pace + opponent pace). Points = possessions × points per possession built from the four factors. Ratings are recency-weighted (half-life ≈110 days) and shrunk toward last season's ratings, scaled by returning minutes.

Players. Each rotation player gets a shrunk game-score-per-40 value over a replacement level. A missing rotation player lowers his team's expected points by about 0.3 pts per unit of missing value (fitted from historical absences). Absences come from data/injuries.csv (team, player, status) plus an auto flag for rotation players who sat out the team's last game (50% weight).

Parlays. One leg per game (no same-game parlays). Each leg's win chance is the model's cover/over probability shrunk toward 50% (default 0.35), capped at 57%, and legs with edges above 10 pts are ignored because large disagreements with the market usually mean missing information. Parlay hit odds multiply the legs, so any model error compounds; stakes are fractional Kelly (small ticket) or a fixed small % of bankroll (lotto). All of this assumes the edges are real, which is not yet verified against closing lines.

Honest limits. Walk-forward backtest 2023-26 (23k games, no look-ahead): margin RMSE 11.8 (naive 15.0), total RMSE 17.1 (naive 19.0). No historical betting lines were available, so profitability against the market is unverified; closing lines are typically sharper than this model.
